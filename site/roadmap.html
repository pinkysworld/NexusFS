Roadmap

Every milestone stays runnable.

NexusFS is staged as a vertical-slice project so the repository remains a usable system at each step, not a pile of disconnected prototypes.

Execution order

  • Finish M1 by completing persistent local namespace state.
  • Ship one real external facade in M2.
  • Complete peer replication in M3.
  • Layer in encryption and transparent proofs in M4.
  • Add energy-aware scheduling and operational hardening next.
  • Leave ZK-specific work until the practical baseline is dependable.
